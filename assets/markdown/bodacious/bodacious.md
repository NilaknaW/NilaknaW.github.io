# 𝗪𝗶𝗻𝗻𝗲𝗿 𝗼𝗳 𝗦𝗿𝗶 𝗟𝗮𝗻𝗸𝗮’𝘀 𝗙𝗶𝗿𝘀𝘁 𝗜𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗿-𝗨𝗻𝗶𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘀𝗶𝘁𝘆 𝗠𝗮𝗸𝗲-𝗔-𝗧𝗵𝗼𝗻 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝟯𝗗-𝗣𝗿𝗶𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗱 𝗔𝘀𝘀𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗠𝗲𝗱𝗶𝗰𝗮𝗹 𝗧𝗲𝗰𝗵𝗻𝗼𝗹𝗼𝗴𝘆!

Our team and I won the first-ever Sri Lanka Inter-University 𝗠𝗮𝗸𝗲-𝗔-𝗧𝗵𝗼𝗻 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝟯𝗗-𝗣𝗿𝗶𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗱 𝗔𝘀𝘀𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗠𝗲𝗱𝗶𝗰𝗮𝗹 𝗧𝗲𝗰𝗵𝗻𝗼𝗹𝗼𝗴𝘆, organized by the University of Kelaniya and co-organized by FabLab Shinagawa.

![300 alt text](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NilaknaW/NilaknaW.github.io/main/assets/assets/markdown/bodacious/makeathon2.jpeg)

Our journey in this competition was truly special as we worked with our need-knower, Master Joel, a bright child with severe 𝗰𝗲𝗿𝗲𝗯𝗿𝗮𝗹 𝘃𝗶𝘀𝘂𝗮𝗹 𝗶𝗺𝗽𝗮𝗶𝗿𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁 (CVI) who loves working on computers. Like every child, he wanted to 𝗹𝗲𝗮𝗿𝗻 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗲𝘅𝗽𝗹𝗼𝗿𝗲 the digital world, but he needed a better way to navigate a keyboard.

💡 𝗢𝘂𝗿 𝗦𝗼𝗹𝘂𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻:

🔹 𝗔 𝘁𝗮𝗻𝗴𝗶𝗯𝗹𝗲 𝗸𝗲𝘆𝗯𝗼𝗮𝗿𝗱 𝗮𝗰𝗰𝗲𝘀𝘀𝗼𝗿𝘆 that allows user to touch and identify the keys effortlessly.

🔹 𝗔𝗻 𝗮𝗱𝗷𝘂𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗯𝗹𝗲 𝗸𝗲𝘆𝗯𝗼𝗮𝗿𝗱 𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗻𝗱 with height and tilt adjustments, ensuring a comfortable experience since he prefers to move very close to the keyboard while working.

![300 alt text](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NilaknaW/NilaknaW.github.io/main/assets/assets/markdown/bodacious/makeathon5.jpeg)

I had the chance to work on the following tasks during the development of our solution, _Enanced Keyboard for Visuallu Impaired Individuals_:

🔹Designing and prototyping the keyboard stand using 3D CAD (SolidWorks).

🔹Ensuring modularity, cost-effectiveness, and manufacturability of the design.

🔹Developing the height and angle adjustment mechanism with minimal complexity and improved usability.

🔹Prototyping and iterating based on user feedback from actual individuals with CVI.

Collaborating with the need-knower was an incredible experience, and we were fortunate to work alongside faculty members and student volunteers from the Department of Disability Studies of the Faculty of Medicine.

This competition was more than just innovation—it was about making a real impact on someone’s life. Winning the 𝗪𝗶𝗻𝗻𝗲𝗿 𝗔𝘄𝗮𝗿𝗱 was the cherry on top, but the most rewarding part was seeing the child engage with our creation.

![300 alt text](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NilaknaW/NilaknaW.github.io/main/assets/assets/markdown/bodacious/makeathon8.jpeg)
