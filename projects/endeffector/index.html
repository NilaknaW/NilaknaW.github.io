Robotic End Effector

As part of the fifth semester, our team designed and implemented a robotic end effector for depalletization in supermarket warehouse logistics.

The system integrates environment sensing and precise actuation using a combination of electric motors and pneumatic systems to handle boxes of varying sizes, weights, and textures efficiently.

💡 My key contributions included:

  • Development of the conceptual design
  • Selection of motor–motor driver pair for the movable bottom rack
  • Design and implementation of the pneumatic subsystem, including suction and piston units.
  • 3D modeling components in SolidWorks, with an extended design for future implementation.
  • Designing the PCB for the power supply circuit.
  • Soldering and assembling the final circuit.
  • Initial testing of UART communication with distance sensors.
  • Conducting design validation and iterative improvements.

This project gave us valuable hands-on experience in mechanical design, control integration, and interdisciplinary teamwork, helping us bridge the gap between classroom theory and real-world engineering challenges.

Project repository: https://github.com/KavishkaJayakody/End_Effector
