01 // About

Where hardware meets interaction

I'm a research intern at the Exertion Games Lab, Monash University, a lab at the forefront of human-computer interaction. I'm also an undergraduate at the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka, pursuing a Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Electronic & Telecommunication Engineering (GPA 3.94, Dean's List).

I like to work at the intersection of hardware and human experience. I design and build analog circuits, FPGA and digital designs, PCBs, and embedded systems, spanning sensing, actuation, and system-level prototyping.

I'm drawn to HCI research because it explores fundamental questions about how we interact, while also creating complex engineering challenges in sensing and interaction. I am especially interested in work that pushes end-to-end hardware systems and signal processing, with strong technical contributions in real-world interactive systems.
